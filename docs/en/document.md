# Document

The document is made from the corrected transcript with a template chosen in the Document tab: «Підсумки доповідачів» (a summary per speaker and an overall summary), «Рішення та завдання» (decisions and tasks), «Доповіді за полями» (each speaker by fields), «Стенограма» (the transcript) or your own. Each document made is kept under its template: the template button above the document shows those already made and makes a new one with another template. When the transcript was corrected after the document was made, «Оновити документ» (Update document) appears above it.

The icon to the right of a name copies that block. Save as DOCX or PDF. Templates are set up in Settings → «Шаблони документа» (Document templates).
